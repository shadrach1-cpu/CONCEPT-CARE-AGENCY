Feedback & complaints

If something is not right, tell us

We want to hear about anything that could be better, whether you are a provider, a carer, or a member of the public.

Please contact us by phone, email or the form above and tell us what happened. We will take your concern seriously, look into it fairly and let you know what we find.

If your concern is about the safety or wellbeing of a person, please see our safeguarding page. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
