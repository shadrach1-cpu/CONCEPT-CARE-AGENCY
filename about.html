About Concept Care Agency

Care staffing built on trust

We connect skilled, caring people with the hospitals, care homes, disability services and support care settings that need them, and we take pride in doing it properly.

Who we are

A local agency with care at its core

Concept Care Agency is a healthcare staffing agency based in Norwich. We supply caregivers, healthcare assistants and support workers to hospitals, care homes, disability services and support caregiving placements across Norfolk.

We know how much depends on getting staffing right. For providers, a reliable carer means safe, consistent care and one less thing to worry about. For carers, a good agency means fair treatment, real support and work that fits their life.

That is the balance we aim to strike every day: looking after the people who need care, the organisations who provide it, and the caregivers who make it happen.

Our values

What guides every placement

Compassion

We put kindness first, for the people receiving care and for the people giving it.

Safety

Safeguarding and thorough pre-employment checks come before everything else.

Reliability

When we confirm a shift, you can count on us. Communication is prompt and honest.

Respect

Dignity, choice and independence for every person in care, and fairness for every carer.
