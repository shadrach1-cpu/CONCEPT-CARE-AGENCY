Getting started

From first call to first shift

We will guide you through every step and answer your questions along the way.

What you will need

Have these ready

  • Right to work in the UK, for example a passport or a Home Office share code.
  • Photo ID and proof of address dated within the last three months.
  • Your National Insurance number.
  • Details of previous employers for references, and any training certificates you already hold.
  • Willingness to complete an enhanced DBS check. We will guide you through it.
Common questions

Good to know

Do I need care experience?

Experience is valued, but not always essential. If you are kind, reliable and willing to learn, get in touch and tell us about yourself.

Do I need to already have a DBS check?

No. We will arrange and guide you through the enhanced DBS check as part of joining us.

Will I get paid holiday?

Yes. Agency workers build up paid holiday in line with UK law. Ask us for details on how this works when we speak.

Is pay at least the legal minimum?

Always. Pay is at or above the National Minimum Wage or National Living Wage that applies to you. We will tell you the rate for each placement before you accept it.

Can I choose when I work?

Yes. You decide which shifts to accept, and we will match you with work that fits your availability.

Can I apply if I need visa sponsorship?

We can only place people who already have the right to work in the UK.
