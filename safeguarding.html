Safeguarding & safer recruitment

Keeping people safe comes first

Everyone we support has the right to live free from abuse, neglect and harm. Here is how we make that real.

Our recruitment process

Six steps before a carer is placed

No carer starts work with us until every step is complete.

  1. Application & interview

    We meet every applicant to understand their experience, values and motivation for care work.

  2. Identity & right to work

    We verify identity and the right to work in the UK in line with Home Office requirements.

  3. Enhanced DBS check

    An enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service check, including the adult barred list, for work with vulnerable adults.

  4. References & employment history

    We take up references and ask about any gaps in employment history.

  5. Training

    Mandatory training is completed and kept up to date, and we check certificates.

  6. Placement & feedback

    We brief the carer on the setting and gather feedback after placements to keep standards high.

Training

Core training for every carer

Training is tailored to the placement, and typically includes:

  • Safeguarding adults
  • Moving and handling
  • Infection prevention & control
  • Fire safety
  • Health & safety
  • Basic life support / first aid
  • Dementia awareness
  • Mental Capacity Act & DoLS
  • Medication awareness
  • Food hygiene & nutrition
  • Equality & diversity
  • Data protection & confidentiality

Our safeguarding commitment

All of our carers are trained to recognise the signs of abuse and neglect and know that they must report concerns immediately, without fear of blame. We work with the placement's safeguarding lead and the local authority safeguarding team where needed.

Equality & respect

We are committed to equal opportunities in line with the Equality Act 2010, and to treating every person we support, and every carer we work with, with dignity and respect.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. To raise a safeguarding concern with us, call 07438 811614 or email contact@concepthealthcareagency.co.uk. Concerns can also be raised confidentially, and we will treat them seriously.

We also encourage carers and providers to tell us about anything that does not feel right. See feedback & complaints.
